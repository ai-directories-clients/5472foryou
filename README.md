# 5472forYOU

Form 5472 filing for foreign-owned US LLCs.

5472forYOU is a paid professional filing service that prepares Form 5472 and the required pro forma Form 1120 for foreign-owned US LLCs. A US tax professional prepares and reviews each filing; pricing is $199 (Standard) or $299 (Expert) per filing, with no subscription.
